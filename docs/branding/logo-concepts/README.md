# FairShare logo exploration

These are raster concept boards for choosing a visual direction. They are not final production vectors yet.

## Concepts

1. **Equal Exchange** (`01-equal-exchange.png`)
   - Two interlocking forms create an implied equals sign.
   - Brand idea: fair, two-way exchange and uncomplicated settlement.

2. **Common Pool** (`02-common-pool.png`)
   - Four rounded forms gather around a shared center.
   - Brand idea: friends contributing to and sharing one common pool.

3. **Settle Path** (`03-settle-path.png`)
   - Two balanced paths separate and resolve into a subtle check.
   - Brand idea: split, track, and settle with calm forward motion.

4. **FS Ribbon** (`04-fs-ribbon.png`)
   - A custom folded-ribbon monogram combines the letters F and S.
   - Brand idea: an ownable brand signature that still hints at two equal halves.

## Prompt set

All concepts used the `logo-brand` image-generation mode and the existing FairShare palette: forest `#173F3A`, coral `#F16F4F`, and warm off-white `#F7F8F4`.

The shared brief requested a crisp, minimal, vector-style identity for a friendly consumer expense-sharing app; the exact lowercase wordmark `fairshare`; a large mark, horizontal lockup, and small app-icon test; a strong silhouette at 24px; and no coins, currency symbols, receipts, pie charts, scales, generic stock symbols, 3D effects, watermarks, or extra copy.

The concept-specific prompts were:

- **Equal Exchange:** two rounded interlocking links forming a subtle equals sign in negative space, with gentle forward motion.
- **Common Pool:** four soft corner shapes gathering around a small central diamond, suggesting friends contributing and receiving a fair part.
- **Settle Path:** one continuous path dividing into two balanced routes and rejoining as a subtle check, expressing split, track, and settle.
- **FS Ribbon:** a custom lowercase F/S monogram built from one folded ribbon, with negative space suggesting two equal halves.

## Recommendation

Start refinement with **Equal Exchange** for the clearest product story or **FS Ribbon** for the most ownable identity. Rebuild the selected mark as a true SVG, correct optical spacing, test it at 16/24/48px, and create monochrome and reversed variants before replacing the existing app icon.
