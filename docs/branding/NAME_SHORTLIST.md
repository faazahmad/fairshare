# Naming shortlist

Research date: 2026-09-27

## Recommendation

Retire **FairShare** before public launch. The exact name is already used by several current expense-splitting products, including multiple mobile apps in the same category. That makes differentiation, search visibility, app-store discovery, and trademark clearance unnecessarily difficult.

## Cleanest coined candidates

These candidates had no obvious exact-name company, software, app-store, or trademark result in the preliminary web screen. Their `.com` names also returned no A or NS record during a DNS check. This is an initial collision screen, not legal clearance or a promise that a domain can still be registered.

### 1. Settelyra

- Pronunciation: **SET-eh-leer-ah**
- Construction: **settle + Lyra**, the constellation.
- Brand story: every scattered expense resolves into one clear constellation.
- Character: modern, celestial, premium, still connected to the product.
- Suggested line: **Everything finds its balance.**

### 2. Equoralis

- Pronunciation: **ee-kwoh-RAH-liss**
- Construction: **equal + aurora/borealis**.
- Brand story: many individual movements becoming one ordered, luminous whole.
- Character: the most elevated and memorable; less literal than Settelyra.
- Suggested line: **Balance, beautifully.**

### 3. Settelys

- Pronunciation: **SET-eh-liss**
- Construction: **settle + Elysium**.
- Brand story: settling shared money should feel effortless and peaceful.
- Character: concise, polished, product-relevant, premium.
- Suggested line: **Settle into balance.**

### 4. Aequonyx

- Pronunciation: **EE-kwoh-niks**
- Construction: Latin **aequus** (equal/fair) + **onyx**.
- Brand story: fairness with strength and permanence.
- Character: dark, confident, luxury-fintech; slightly harder to spell.
- Suggested line: **Fairness, set in stone.**

## Screening caveat

An exact-string internet search cannot establish worldwide exclusivity. A real clearance must also check visually, phonetically, and conceptually similar marks in relevant jurisdictions and software/financial-service classes, followed by domain and corporate-name registration at the moment of filing.

## Names rejected during the screen

- **Divyra** — already an expense-splitting app.
- **Evenora** — already used by a software marketplace/event platform and appears in trademark records.
- **Equira** — already a live finance/portfolio application.
- **Sethera** — active biotechnology company.
- **Quoriva** — active technology/research brands.
- **Tulyra** — active trademark filings/registrations.
- **Parithea**, **Sumvera**, **Aeqora**, **Elysum**, **Pactara**, and **Evenyx** — visible existing commercial uses.
