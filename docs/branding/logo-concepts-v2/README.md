# FairShare logo exploration — round two

These are raster concept boards produced with the built-in image generator. They are for direction selection, not final production assets.

## Directions

1. **Celestial Equilibrium** — opposing equal arcs around a centered resolving point.
2. **FS Gate** — architectural F/S monogram with an embedded equality motif.
3. **Shared Star** — equal facets producing a centered celestial form in negative space.
4. **The Fair Cut** — wordmark-first identity with a precise division between `fair` and `share`.
5. **FS Gate Refined** — tighter small-size version of direction two.
6. **The Fair Cut Refined** — corrected and simplified version of direction four.

## Critical review

- **Strongest symbol:** FS Gate Refined.
- **Strongest complete identity:** The Fair Cut Refined.
- **Rejected:** Celestial Equilibrium reads too close to yin-yang; Shared Star is too generic to own confidently.

## Prompt set

The shared brief asked for a modern, premium, iconic FairShare identity connected to equal division and shared settlement; exact lowercase text `fairshare`; strict small-size performance; restrained celestial or sacred-geometric precision without religious imagery; and no receipt, coin, currency, pie-chart, hand, scale, generic-person, 3D, mockup, or watermark clichés.

The four concept-specific prompts explored:

- two equal opposing arcs with a resolving central point;
- an architectural F/S monogram with a hidden equals sign;
- equal facets forming a shared star through negative space;
- a custom wordmark divided by one precise diagonal cut.

The two refinement prompts preserved only the FS Gate and Fair Cut ideas, simplified their geometry, requested exact wordmark spelling, and required flat two-color construction suitable for an eventual SVG rebuild.
